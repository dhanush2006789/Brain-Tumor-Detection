About Medical Image Segmentation

Image segmentation in the medical field involves partitioning medical images into multiple segments or regions to identify objects and boundaries, enabling detailed analysis of anatomical structures.

Clinical Applications

Medical image segmentation is crucial for disease diagnosis, treatment planning, and surgical navigation. It's widely used in radiology, oncology, cardiology, and neurology.

Segmentation Techniques

Our platform employs advanced ML models including U-Net, SegNet, and transformer-based architectures to achieve high-precision segmentation across different imaging modalities.

Supported Modalities

We support MRI scans and provide versatile analysis capabilities.

U-Net

U-Net is a convolutional neural network designed for biomedical image segmentation, featuring an encoder-decoder structure that captures context and enables precise localization.

SegNet

SegNet uses an encoder-decoder architecture with pooling indices for efficient segmentation, providing accurate pixel-wise classification with reduced computational cost.
